- **Phase 2 choices.** Price features are computed on each stock's full price history, then masked to member days. volz = z-score of log(1+volume) vs the trailing 20 days incl. today. Daily sentiment = mean FinBERT score of the headlines assigned to that trade day (0 + has_news=0 if none); events = any headline that day. `sent_surprise` = today's score minus the mean of the stock's news-day scores over the previous 60 trading days (t-60..t-1), and 0 on no-news days or with no past news. Alternative (rejected): trailing mean including zero no-news days, which mostly measures news frequency.
- **Regimes:** market features = SPY 1/5/20-day return, SPY 20-day vol, cross-sectional std of member returns. Standardise + PCA (2 PCs, ~63% var) + 2-state full-cov GaussianHMM (best of 5 seeds), all fit on train dates only, filtered with own forward pass (`regimes.forward_filter`) over train+test using train params. State 1 = high-vol. Feature = p_high. 2 states keeps the sentiment x regime interaction to one term per sentiment variable. Alternative: 3 states (more parameters, less stable fold to fold).
- **Fold purge:** train rows also need next_date < test start, so the last pre-test day (whose label uses a test-year return) is excluded from training.
- **Leakage tests** (`tests/test_leakage.py`, 13 pass): target shift changes predictions; every feature |corr(f_t,y)| < 0.05 and not > 0.02 above its lag (with a planted-leak control that the check must flag); strict fold dates; regime fit unaffected by changing test-period data; forward filter truncation-invariant; no predict_proba; 2023 absent from every input file and enforce_seal drops it; no pipeline file reads news_sealed.
- **Phase 3 choices.** Logit inputs are clipped at train 1/99 pct and z-scored with train stats. p_high stays raw (0-1) and interactions = z(sent) x p_high and z(surprise) x p_high, so the `sent` coefficient = effect in the low-vol regime and sent + sent_x_high = effect in the high-vol regime. statsmodels Logit with SEs clustered by date (cross-sectional correlation). n_news enters as log1p. Event dummies with zero training variance are dropped from that fold. H2/H3 inference uses each fold's training fit (2022 fold = 2010-21 sample is the largest), and H2 is also tested out of sample (M3 vs M2 paired log loss). LightGBM fixed params: 300 trees, lr .05, 31 leaves, min_child_samples 1000, subsample/colsample .8, lambda 1. Placebo shuffles the whole sentiment+event block across stocks within each date, in train AND test (seeded).
- **Li-7 = same models, evaluated on the 7 tickers' rows** (no refit). Li-7 portfolio: with ~7 stocks a decile = k = max(1, round(0.1 n)) = 1 long / 1 short. Costs: 10 bps x daily turnover sum|w_t - w_{t-1}| over both legs (gross exposure 2), so a full daily rebalance costs ~20 bps/day. NW lags = 5 for all t-stats.
- **Phase 3b LSTM.** 1-layer LSTM (hidden 32) on the M3 inputs (same train-fold preprocessing), 20-row windows of the stock's own consecutive panel rows (member days only; features only, never labels, from before t). AdamW lr 1e-3, wd 1e-4, batch 4096, max 15 epochs, patience 3; validation = last 20% of training dates, purged (fit rows need next_date < validation start). Seed 340+year. Rows without 20 prior rows get no LSTM prediction (573,318 of 574,824 test rows); paired tests/portfolios use rows where both models predict. Ran in <1 min total, so well within the 19:00/22:00 gates.

## Session 3d (2026-11-07 14:03-14:15): relevance-filter robustness (M2_rel/M3_rel), added before 2023, motivated by the annotation audit
- **Why:** results/hand_check_agreement.md: 34 of 94 FinBERT/LLM disagreements were headlines about another company than the tagged ticker.
- **Rule** (`src/relevance.py`): keep a headline for its ticker if it contains (a) the symbol, whole-word and case-sensitive; for 1-2-letter symbols only "$T", "(T)" or ":T"; or (b) a company name, case-insensitive on word boundaries. Names = universe.csv `name` + SEC entity names of the ticker's CIK(s) incl. predecessor CIKs and formerNames in use in 2009+ (older ones like KeyCorp's "Society" or AT&T's "SBC" add false positives). Suffixes removed: inc, corp, co, company, holdings, ltd, limited, plc, llc, lp, nv, sa, ag, se, group, the, class a, state tags, "/..." SEC tags. Short variants: name minus one trailing descriptor (motors, technologies, industries, systems, international, pharmaceuticals, financial, energy, ...), and the first word of 4+ letters.
